# Changelog

All notable changes to PSAK ID are listed here. The project follows [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/).

## [Unreleased]

### Added

- PSAK ID Report Line: the statement of financial position and other comprehensive income. The standard lines of both (PRD-002 Appendices B and E) are installed, with total assets, total equity, total liabilities and equity and total comprehensive income required. Financial position lines are assets, liabilities or equity, and assets and liabilities are current or non-current; other comprehensive income lines are marked as may or will not be reclassified to profit or loss. A subtotal formula can add the current or non-current lines of a category (`+CAT:Asset/Current`) and the subtotal of another statement (`+PL-SUB-PL`).
- PSAK ID Account Mapping: asset, liability and equity accounts are mapped to a financial position line of their own category. Equity accounts also name their equity component (by default their line) and, for reserves of other comprehensive income, the line their movements are presented in. Mapping a cash or bank account anywhere but cash and cash equivalents, or another account there, shows a warning.
- Mapping Control: a balance sheet account with a balance at the end of the period and no mapping in force then is a blocking issue.

## [1.0.3] - 2026-11-25

### Added

- A release published on GitHub is advertised on the Frappe Marketplace: the `publish-marketplace` workflow adds it to the registry from a fork and opens or updates one rolling PR to `frappe/marketplace`. It needs the `MARKETPLACE_PR_TOKEN` secret.

### Changed

- Mapping Control and PSAK ID Financial Statement: conditional mappings of the same priority that put an account's balance on different lines block the statement from being sent for review, since which line gets the balance is arbitrary. They stay a warning when the account's entries in the period net to nothing.
- PSAK ID Financial Statement: the Accounting Head no longer creates statements. The Head reviews what staff prepare and could not edit a draft, so a statement the Head created could not be computed. Give one person both the Staff and the Head roles where the same person prepares and reviews.

## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-24

Release 1: the PSAK 118 statement of profit or loss (PRD-001).

### Fixed

- PSAK 118 Profit or Loss: clicking an amount whose account a conditional mapping splits between lines opens the General Ledger for that line's cost centers or projects, so it shows the entries behind the amount instead of every entry of the account. When one set of filters cannot select the line's entries (its other accounts need the cost centers the split leaves out), a dialog lists the line's accounts with their amounts and opens the General Ledger for one of them. Where the General Ledger cannot select them at all (a split by party or another accounting dimension, or a total over several periods), an alert says that it also shows entries of other lines.
- PSAK ID Financial Statement: a new statement takes its unit from the company policy. The form used to start on Full, so the policy's default unit was never used.
- PSAK ID Financial Statement: once Other Items above the threshold are explained in Other Items Breakdown, the banner turns green instead of still asking for the breakdown.
- **Download > PDF** of a cancelled (reopened) version: it prints with its CANCELLED watermark even where Print Settings keep cancelled documents from printing, which is Frappe's default. Draft versions likewise print with DRAFT whatever Print Settings allow.
- PDF: the rounding and restatement footnotes stay on the page of the last totals of their table; they no longer end up alone on a page.
- PSAK ID - Auditor reads the change history (Version) of the PSAK ID documents they can read, as the PRD's role matrix says, through **Change History** in the PSAK 118 sidebar. The rule is added as a Custom DocPerm on Version on install and by a patch, limited by permission hooks to versions of PSAK ID documents the user can read, and removed on uninstall; users who read Version through another role see what they saw before.
- PSAK ID Financial Statement: To Date is again the last day of the period that starts on From Date. It was set to the end of the current month, because `frappe.datetime.month_end()` ignores its argument.
- PSAK 118 Profit or Loss and PSAK ID Mapping Control open on last month again, as intended, instead of the current month.
- PSAK ID Financial Statement: **View > PSAK 118 Profit or Loss** and **View > Mapping Control** no longer pass an empty finance book as the text "undefined", which showed a permission error and left the report empty until it was reloaded.

### Changed

- README describes release 1: its features, the roles and the ERPNext role each user also needs, a getting started sequence, and what to know about adjusting the ledger (post audit adjustments before the next year's statements), dated mappings and reopening.
- CI runs the Frappe v15 tests on Python 3.11: Frappe 15.128 no longer installs on Python 3.10.

### Added

- Workspace "PSAK 118" (FR-11), opened from the PSAK ID tile on the apps screen (only for users who can read a statement or company policy). Number cards that only count rows: statements awaiting review, awaiting CFO approval, final statements whose period ends this year, and income and expense accounts of PSAK ID companies with no mapping in force today (no GL Entry is read). Shortcuts to a new statement, Mapping Control and ERPNext's Profit and Loss Statement, the latest statements, and link cards for Reports, Mapping and Settings. On Frappe v16 a module sidebar lists Home; Reports (Financial Statements, Profit or Loss); Mapping (Mapping Control, Account Mapping, Report Lines, Mapping Templates); Settings (Company Policy, Change History: versions of PSAK ID documents). Items a user cannot read are hidden. v15 uses the workspace's link cards; it ignores the sidebar file. A user who cannot create a statement, such as the auditor, is taken from the new statement shortcut to the statement list instead of an empty form.
- **Download > Excel** on the PSAK ID Financial Statement (FR-10): sheets Laba Rugi, Beban per Sifat and Rekonsiliasi Transisi when the statement has them, and Info (company, period, finance book, status, document, version, preparer, reviewer, approver, export time). Same bilingual title block and wording as the PDF, centred across the table without merged cells, English labels in their own column, frozen column headers, fixed column widths, A4 portrait fitted to one page wide with repeated headers and page numbers. Amounts are stored in full and shown in the statement's unit by the number format; subtotals and totals are formulas over the cells of the lines they add up (a subtotal left off the statement is expanded into its own lines), so they give the statement's amounts and still add up when a line is changed. A subtotal whose report line formula changed after the statement was computed is written as its stored amount.
- **Download > Structured Data** (Excel or CSV, BR-14): one row per line with fixed English columns `line_code`, `label_id`, `label_en`, `category`, `line_type`, `period_from`, `period_to`, `amount`, `comparative_amount`, `psak201_amount`, `unit`, `currency`, `company`, `statement_name`, `status`; amounts in full with a decimal point, ISO dates, UTF-8 CSV with commas. `unit` is the statement's presentation unit; `psak201_amount` is filled in the transition year from the transition reconciliation.
- Print Format "PSAK 118 Financial Statement" and **Download > PDF** on the PSAK ID Financial Statement (FR-10): A4 portrait, bilingual (Indonesian with English below), the company policy's letter head on page 1, then a running header, the table header repeated on each page, page numbers, document name, version and print time in the footer with the standard notes sentence. Sections start on a new page: statement of profit or loss, operating expenses by nature, transition reconciliation, Other Items breakdown and an Approval Sheet with the preparer, reviewer and approver, their function and time. Amounts in the statement's unit, dot thousands, negatives in brackets, zero as a dash; restated comparative columns point to the transition reconciliation. Marked unaudited; watermarked DRAFT until Final and CANCELLED once reopened. Frappe renders PDFs with JavaScript off, so the download adds the running header (pages 2 on) and the watermark to the rendered pages and has wkhtmltopdf print the page numbers; the standard Print view shows the same layout without them. The template lives in `templates/print_formats/`.
- PSAK ID Financial Statement: Prepared On and Reviewed On record when the statement was submitted for review and sent to the CFO.
- PSAK ID Financial Statement: expenses by nature (BR-05, R-06). For a company that presents expenses by function or mixed, Recompute lists each nature (raw materials and goods, employee benefits, depreciation, amortisation, impairment losses, inventory write-downs, other) with the amount included in each Operating function line, for the period and the comparative, from the expense nature of each account mapping. The statement engine now returns these amounts (`natures`).
- PSAK ID Financial Statement, first PSAK 118 year: a transition reconciliation of the prior year from PSAK 201 lines to PSAK 118 lines (BR-11). PSAK 201 amounts are ERPNext's Profit and Loss; each account takes the PSAK 201 line of the PSAK 118 line it is mapped to, so one PSAK 201 line may split over several PSAK 118 lines, and accounts with no mapping are shown on their own. The statement records the prior year's PSAK 201 and restated profit or loss side by side; they differ by exactly the unmapped amount, which blocks review (BR-19). Stored rows are frozen with the statement at Final (BR-14).
- PSAK ID Financial Statement (submittable): a PSAK 118 statement of profit or loss for one company and period (whole months: monthly, quarterly or annual), finance book, comparative (none, same period last year, or the prior year restated, which the first PSAK 118 year requires) and unit. Saving or **Recompute** fills one row per presented line with its amount, comparative amount and an editable note number that survives recomputing, and records the Mapping Control checks: unmapped accounts, the difference to ERPNext's Profit and Loss, the restatement difference and Other Items lines above the threshold; the form shows them as a red, orange or green banner and links to Mapping Control and the PSAK 118 Profit or Loss report. One statement per company, period and finance book. Named `PSAK-<company abbreviation>-<YYYY-MM>-<nn>`.
- Workflow PSAK ID Financial Statement (fixture): Draft or Reopened, Under Review, Approval CFO, Final, Cancelled. Submit for Review recomputes from the ledger and is refused while a check fails or an Other Items line above the threshold has no breakdown; sending back asks for a reason, recorded as a comment. Approve is refused until the period is closed in ERPNext (closed Accounting Periods covering it, or a Period Closing Voucher for an annual statement) and when the ledger changed after review. Preparer, reviewer, approver and approval time are recorded. A final statement is not cancelled: **Reopen** (Accounting Head or CFO) asks for a reason, cancels it and keeps it, and creates an amendment in Reopened with the reason, user and time.
- PSAK ID Account Mapping: a mapping in force on or before the end of the last final statement of the company cannot be added, reclassified or deleted; notes stay editable.
- PSAK ID Mapping Template: ordered rows of account name or number patterns (`*` matches any text, case ignored), optionally only for income or expense accounts, each with a report line, expense nature and final tax flag. The first matching row maps an account. Rows to subtotals or disabled lines, final tax to Income Tax and repeated patterns are refused. **Apply to Company** creates a mapping for each unmapped income and expense account the template matches, valid from a chosen date (the company's adoption date by default), skips accounts that already have a mapping, and shows what was created, skipped, not matched or refused. Every mapping records its source template and version.
- Five standard templates (Services, Trading, Manufacturing, Property, Investment Holding) covering ERPNext's standard chart and common Indonesian account names; the Services template covers project costs (technicians, subcontractors, consumables, equipment rent and depreciation, mobilisation), interest on deposits and its final tax in Investing, and exchange differences on trade balances in Operating. Installed on new sites and by a patch on existing ones, never overwriting a template the user changed.
- Script Report "PSAK ID Mapping Control": lists what must be fixed before a PSAK 118 statement can be sent for review, for a company, period and finance book. Blocking: accounts with a balance and no mapping, entries matching none of an account's conditional mappings with no fallback, accounts mapped to a disabled line, a difference to ERPNext's Profit and Loss and, in the first PSAK 118 year, accounts and a difference in the prior year restated with this year's mappings. Warnings: Other Items lines above the company threshold (the smaller of the percentage of revenue and the amount) and conditional mappings that match the same entries at one priority but name different lines. Each row opens what fixes it (a new mapping prefilled with the account, the mappings of the account or line, or ERPNext's Profit and Loss for the period). The checks live in `psak_id/checks.py` for the Financial Statement to reuse. A year of 500,000 GL Entries with the restated prior year checks in under 3 seconds on the Dev bench.
- Statement engine: unmapped amounts state why they have no line (not mapped, no matching condition, disabled line), and conditional mappings tied at one priority are reported as conflicts.
- Script Report "PSAK 118 Profit or Loss": monthly, quarterly or yearly columns with a total, a comparative for the same period last year or the prior year restated with this year's mappings, cost center and finance book filters, amounts in full, thousands or millions (company default when left empty), Indonesian, English or both labels, optional category headings, negatives in brackets and zero as a dash. The summary compares the profit or loss with ERPNext's Profit and Loss net profit, unmapped accounts are named, and clicking an amount opens the General Ledger for the line's accounts and period. Printing uses the statement layout, always marked DRAFT and unaudited. A year of 500,000 GL Entries in monthly columns with a restated comparative runs in under 3 seconds on the Dev bench.
- Statement engine: `compute_periods()` computes several periods in one pass over the General Ledger, every period classified with the mappings in force on one date; lines moved to Operating are presented above the Operating subtotal.
- PSAK 118 statement engine (`psak_id/statement.py`): sums GL Entries per account and per value of the dimensions that mapping conditions use, resolves each group to a report line (matching conditional mapping with the lowest priority number, else the unconditional fallback), moves lines to Operating for a main business of investing or customer financing, evaluates the subtotal formulas and lists unmapped accounts. The GL query applies the same conditions as ERPNext's Profit and Loss Statement, so its ledger total is ERPNext's net profit (tests compare the two on v15 and v16), and the statement reports any difference to it. One year of 500,000 GL Entries computes in about 2 seconds on the Dev bench. Reads the ledger only.
- PSAK ID Account Mapping: maps each income or expense account of a company to a report line, optionally only for one cost center, project, party or accounting dimension value, with a priority, a start date, the expense nature for the by-nature note and a final tax flag. Rejects accounts of another company, group or balance sheet accounts, non-line targets, final tax mapped to Income Tax, operating expenses without a nature and a second identical rule. Changes are tracked.
- PSAK ID Report Line: tree of statement lines with Indonesian and English labels, PSAK 118 category, sequence, subtotal formula (`+PL-OP-REV -PL-OP-COS +CAT:Operating`), Other Items flag, follows-Operating flag and PSAK 201 line. The standard PSAK 118 statement of profit or loss is inserted on install; the three required subtotals cannot be disabled, deleted or redefined, formulas are checked for unknown lines and loops, and a line used in a formula cannot be deleted.
- Four PSAK ID roles (Accounting - Staff, Accounting - Head, CFO, Auditor) shipped as fixtures.
- PSAK ID Company Policy: one per company, with the reporting framework, PSAK 118 adoption date, main business activities, expense presentation, Other Items threshold, default unit and letter head that later statements use.
- Instructions for AI coding assistants (`AGENTS.md`, `CLAUDE.md`, `docs/agents/`, shared slash commands) and pointer stubs to the maintainers' PRDs, UAT docs and Memory Bank.
- gettext translations like ERPNext: `locale/main.pot` plus PO files for Indonesian, Simplified Chinese, Japanese and Arabic (English is the source). CI fails when they are stale.
- App skeleton for Frappe / ERPNext v15 and v16: module `PSAK ID`, CI on both versions, linters and the Frappe Marketplace Semgrep gate.
- PSAK ID logo (`public/images/psak-id-logo.svg`) for the apps screen and README, and a full-bleed square variant (`public/images/psak-id-marketplace.svg`) for the Frappe Marketplace listing that Pilot shows.
